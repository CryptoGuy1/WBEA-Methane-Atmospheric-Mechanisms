# Caption: e14_yearswap_null (Supplementary Material)
**Empirical cross-year negative control for the frozen directional (H2) test, development data 2021–2025.**
**Categories:**
- **Cross-year:** calendar-aligned methane and wind drawn from *different* development years (20 combinations), used as empirical negative controls.
- **Same-year:** methane and wind from the *same* observed year (5 combinations), retaining their observed alignment. These are development data and are not evidence of physical transport.

**Panels:**
- **(a)** Standardised pooled statistic z under the frozen whole-period shifted-wind reference (246 whole-day shifts). The one cross-year combination with p <= 0.05 is outlined in orange. Horizontal positions within each category are small display offsets by year. The dashed line marks the registered 2026 confirmation statistic (z = 6.99) **for comparison only; it is not part of the cross-year negative-control experiment.**
- **(b)** Empirical cumulative distribution of the 20 cross-year finite shifted-wind p-values for all eligible stations and for the 2026 station set (AMS 2 removed). The dotted diagonal is the uniform reference; the vertical line marks p = 0.05.
- **(c)** Monthly standard deviation of hourly leave-pair-out residual changes (network mean over pairs) for each development year (grey) and their mean (black). It shows the calendar-recurrent seasonal structure retained by cross-year combinations.

**Note:** cross-year combinations reuse five methane and five wind years, so proportions and distributions are descriptive. They do not constitute a calibration or Type-I error estimate.
