# E08 amendments after independent review (2026-11-10)
Frozen results, code, tags and decisions are unchanged. These amendments concern reporting and interpretation only.

1. **H3 weight concentration.** The registered pooled projection share (0.011, 95% CI -0.020 to 0.043) is retained. Its denominator is dominated by AMS 18 (92.9% of sum(t^2) in 2026; 39.0% in development Jan-Sep). A share excluding AMS 18 (-0.085, point only) is post-confirmation exploratory and is not a substitute.
2. **H1 negative control.** The 2026 next-day coefficient is -0.019 (-0.027 to -0.005), unlike development (0.000). The registered decision (confirmed) stands; the discrepancy is reported prominently, with candidate non-causal explanations, none established.
3. **H2 magnitude.** 0.128 (development 2021-2025, E05 weights from 1,000 random shifts, 27 pairs), 0.184 (E08 method on development Jan-Sep 2025, 246 finite shifts, 27 pairs) and 0.288 (2026 Jan-Sep, 21 pairs) differ in window, pair set and null weighting. They are not estimates of transport intensity and are not compared as effect sizes.
4. **AMS 2.** Exclusion (41.2% valid CH4) retained. Official explanation not yet found: WBEA monthly reports for Jan-Sep 2026 (section 3.1 and Station 2 operational notes) and the 2026-27 quarterly progress reports were not reachable through search. Request from WBEA or download from wbea.org (Resources > Regulatory Reporting > Monthly).
5. **H3 wording.** Categorical statements ("weather composition does not explain methane trends") are replaced by the estimand-specific conclusion: the registered pooled projection share of between-year differences in station P95 excursion rates (frozen 2021-2023 thresholds) attributable to changes in the frequency of 18 pre-registered stability x wind x day/night regimes was not distinguishable from zero, and is dominated by AMS 18.
