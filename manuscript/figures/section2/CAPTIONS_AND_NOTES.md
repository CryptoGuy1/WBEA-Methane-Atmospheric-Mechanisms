# Suggested captions (revision 2; captions sit below each figure in LaTeX; no titles inside figures)

**Fig. 1.** Geographical distribution of the Wood Buffalo Environmental Association (WBEA) atmospheric methane monitoring stations in northern Alberta, Canada. Station locations are shown using official monitoring-network coordinates (WBEA site documents, 2024). Lines connecting monitoring stations represent the 21 station pairs eligible for statistical comparison in the 2026 confirmation analysis, not verified methane transport pathways; AMS 2 (open square) was excluded from that analysis because fewer than 50% of its hourly methane values were valid. AMS 18 lies 92 km south of the mapped area. The inset shows the location of the mapped area in Canada. Base data: Natural Earth.

**Fig. 2.** Temporal availability of hourly methane concentration observations at the WBEA monitoring stations from January to September 2026. The colour scale represents the proportion of valid hourly observations within each day; under normal operation one hour per day is used for the automated zero/span check, so a normally operating day has 23 valid hours (96%). Missing measurements (zero/span, calibration, maintenance or invalid hours) are distinguished from valid concentration measurements. Percentages on the right give each station's share of valid hours over the period.

**Fig. S1.** Monthly completeness of hourly methane observations at the 12 stations, January 2021 to November 2025 (development period; recommended version). **Alternative Fig. S1 (daily):** daily availability over the same period, coded as in Fig. 2.
