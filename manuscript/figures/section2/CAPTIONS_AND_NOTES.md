# Suggested captions and table notes

**Fig. 1.** Geographic location and spatial configuration of the Wood Buffalo Environmental Association (WBEA) methane monitoring network in northeastern Alberta, Canada.
- (a) Regional location; the red rectangle marks the study-area extent.
- (b) The 12 monitoring stations considered in the study, with the 21 station pairs eligible for the directional-dependence analysis in the 2026 confirmation period (pairs within 30 km among the 11 stations retained after the pre-specified methane completeness criterion). The dashed box shows the extent of (c).
- (c) Enlarged view of the station cluster along the Athabasca River.

AMS 2 (open grey square) was excluded from the 2026 analysis because fewer than 50% of its hourly methane values were valid; the 2021-2025 development analysis included it (27 pairs). Connecting lines indicate statistical station-pair relationships, not observed methane transport pathways. Boundaries, river, lakes and communities from Natural Earth; station coordinates from WBEA 2024 site documents. Projections: (a) EPSG:3978; (b, c) UTM zone 12N.

**Fig. 2.** Daily availability of valid hourly methane (CH4) observations at the 12 WBEA stations, 1 January - 30 September 2026 (hour-ending Mountain Standard Time). Each cell is one station-day, coloured by the number of valid hourly values (0-24); hours flagged for zero/span checks (Z), calibration (C) or as invalid/maintenance ('.') are counted as missing. Under normal operation one hour per day is used for the automated zero/span check, so a fully operating day has 23 valid hours. Percentages at right give each station's valid share of all 6,552 hours. AMS 2 is labelled because it fell below the pre-specified 50% completeness criterion.

**Fig. S1.** As Fig. 2, for the development period 1 January 2021 - 1 December 2025 (1,796 days). No station was excluded in this period.

**Table 1, note.** As in the table file. Heights list variables with at least 5% valid hours in 2021-2025 exports. The analyser model is verified only for AMS 5.

**Table 2, note.** Development-period analyses used all months; comparisons between development and confirmation years used matching 1 January - 30 September windows.
