# Station relocation investigation (Section 2 revision, 2026-11-11)

**Question:** are the WBEA 2024 coordinates valid for 2021-2026, or was any station relocated?

## Evidence obtained (official WBEA site documents, via web search of wbea.org)
| Station | Evidence | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| AMS 1 Fort McKay - Bertha Ganter | Site documentation (revision 27 Mar 2024): "Date station established: October 1997"; legal land description 13-25-094-11 W4 | Established long before the study period |
| AMS 23 Fort Hills | Site documentation (2024; revision March 2026): "Date station established: March 2017" | Established before the study period |
| AMS 13 Fort McKay South | Site documentation revised 18 Feb 2026 | No relocation statement in the retrieved text |
| All stations | 2023 PDF, March-2024 PDF and 2026 Word revisions of the site documents exist | Multi-year snapshots exist and could be compared |
| AMS 5, AMS 23 | WBEA monthly report Sep 2024: CH4 analyser replacements (11 and 25 Sep 2024) | Instrument changes, not relocations |

## What could not be verified here
- The full documents (2023/2024 PDFs, 2026 Word files and the *Volume III Continuous Data 2025* compilation) are too large to retrieve in this environment because of embedded images. Their coordinates and siting histories could not be compared station by station.
- No WBEA statement of a relocation of any of the 12 stations during 2021-2026 was found, but absence of evidence is not verification.

## Status and recommendation
**Unresolved for 10 of 12 stations; partially supported for AMS 1 and AMS 23** (establishment dates predate the study; no relocation mentioned).

Recommended action: download *Volume III Continuous Data 2025* (https://wbea.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Volume-III-Continuous-Data-2025.pdf) and an earlier edition (for example 2020 or 2022), then compare latitude, longitude and "date established" for the 12 stations. Any difference greater than about 100 m should be reported in Section 2.

Until then, the manuscript should state: "Coordinates are from the 2024 WBEA site documents; no relocations during 2021-2026 were identified in available WBEA documentation, but a complete siting history was not available."
